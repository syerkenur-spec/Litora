# Chapter 2: Daily Routine

## Reading

Every day, Maria wakes up at seven o'clock. She brushes her teeth and eats
breakfast. At eight thirty, she goes to work by bus. She works until five
in the evening. After work, she usually cooks dinner and watches television.
She goes to bed at eleven o'clock.

## Vocabulary

- wake up (phrasal verb) — to stop sleeping
- brush (verb) — to clean with a brush, e.g. teeth
- breakfast (noun) — the first meal of the day
- by bus (phrase) — a means of transportation
- usually (adverb) — most of the time

## Grammar Note

**Present Simple for routines**

We use the present simple to talk about habits and routines, adding -s or
-es for he/she/it:
- I wake up at seven.
- She wakes up at seven.
- They go to work by bus.

Time expressions like "every day", "usually", and "at eight thirty" often
appear with the present simple.
