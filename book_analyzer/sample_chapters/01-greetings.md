# Chapter 1: Greetings

## Dialogue

Anna: Hello! My name is Anna. What is your name?
Tom: Hi Anna, I'm Tom. Nice to meet you.
Anna: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?
Tom: I'm from Canada. And you?
Anna: I'm from Spain.

## Vocabulary

- hello (interjection) — a greeting used when meeting someone
- name (noun) — what a person or thing is called
- nice to meet you (phrase) — a polite expression used when meeting someone for the first time
- from (preposition) — indicating origin or source

## Grammar Note

**To be: am / is / are**

We use the verb "to be" to talk about names and origins.
- I am (I'm) Tom.
- You are from Canada.
- She is from Spain.

Use "am" with I, "is" with he/she/it, and "are" with you/we/they.
